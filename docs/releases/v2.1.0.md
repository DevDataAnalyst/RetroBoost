# RetroBoost 2.1.0: clear cookies, stay signed in 🍪

**The cookie keep-list arrives: pick the sites you use, and the cookie cleaners delete every other site's cookies while you stay signed in where it matters.**

This completes the port of [FluentCleaner](https://github.com/builtbybel/FluentCleaner)'s cleaner features into RetroBoost's retro text UI (2.0 brought APP CLEAN, TWEAKS, UNINSTALL and AUTO). Still free, one readable script, **no install, no admin needed, no network calls, no telemetry**.

## ✨ What's new

- 🍪 **COOKIES** (`C` on APP CLEAN): every site that has cookies in your browsers, read straight from their cookie databases (site names only, read-only, never the cookie values). Subdomains are folded into their site, kept sites sit on top, `SPACE` keeps or forgets a site, and a letter jumps to the sites that start with it.
- ✂️ **An edit, not a wipe.** With sites on the list, a browser's cookie cleaner no longer deletes the cookie database. It deletes every *other* site's cookies inside it and keeps your sites **and their subdomains** (`github.com` keeps `api.github.com`, not `notgithub.com`), then compacts the file. Journal and session files are left alone so kept logins stay valid.
- 🌍 **Every browser winapp2 knows.** RetroBoost finds the cookie databases through winapp2's own cookie rules, so this covers Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc and the other Chromium browsers, plus Firefox, Thunderbird and the other Firefox-family apps, using the SQLite that ships with Windows (`winsqlite3.dll`).
- ⚠️ **No surprises.** The plan screen tells you before anything runs: "keep 3 sites signed in", or, with no list, "every cookie goes: you are signed out of every site". The result screen counts the cookies removed and the sites kept.

## 🛡️ Safe by design

- A cookie database that is busy (the browser is open) or can't be edited is **left whole, never deleted instead**, and the log says why. Any error rolls the change back.
- The cookie cleaners still start **unticked**, and browser cleaners still wait while that browser runs.
- Without a keep-list, nothing changes from 2.0: the rule deletes the cookie file, as winapp2 says.
- Six new sandbox checks (39 in all) build a real SQLite cookie database in a fake Chrome profile and prove the screen, the saved list, the dry run, the edit, the files kept, and the plain delete without a list. CI runs them on Windows.

## 📦 Install or upgrade (2 minutes)

1. Install PowerShell 7 once: `winget install Microsoft.PowerShell`
2. Download **`RetroBoost-v2.1.0.zip`** below. Right-click it → *Properties* → tick **Unblock** → *Extract all*. (Upgrading? Replace the folder; your settings in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RetroBoost` are kept.)
3. Double-click **`RetroBoost.cmd`** → `A` (APP CLEAN) → `C` (COOKIES) → `SPACE` on the sites you use → `ESC`. Then tick a *Web Browsing Cookies* cleaner, close that browser, and press ENTER.

Want a risk-free look first? `RetroBoost.cmd -Demo` (invented sites) or `RetroBoost.cmd -DryRun` (your PC, nothing changed).

## ⚠️ Known limitations

- Sites are grouped by their last two labels (three after `co.uk`-style endings), not the full Public Suffix List. The error is on the safe side: keeping `user.github.io` keeps all of `github.io`.
- No import/export of the list yet, and no "delete this site's cookies now" button (FluentCleaner has both); the list lives in `config.json` as `keepCookies` ([roadmap](https://github.com/DevDataAnalyst/RetroBoost/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md)).
- A browser that is open keeps its cookie database locked: COOKIES shows it as *busy*, and its cleaners wait.
