# Code of Conduct

RetroBoost is for everyone with a PC, so its community should be welcoming to everyone who wants to help.

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, ability, identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, religion or any other characteristic. Beginners are especially welcome: everyone was one once.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good intent; explain things; say thank you.
- **Be respectful** of different opinions, setups and skill levels.
- **Keep feedback constructive:** criticise code and ideas, not people.
- **Keep it on topic** and useful for the project.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal information; remove your own personal paths and names from logs and screenshots you post.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, or promoting unrelated products.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue asking for a private channel (don't include details in the public issue) or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile; reports are handled promptly and discreetly.
